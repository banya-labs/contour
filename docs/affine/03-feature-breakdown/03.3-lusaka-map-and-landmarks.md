# 03.3 — Interactive Lusaka Map & Landmark Directions Navigator

> **Surface Path**: `/dashboard/map` & `/kiosk`  
> **Primary Audience**: Field Real Estate Agents, Viewing Coordinators, Prospective Clients  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Interactive Lusaka Map & Landmark Navigator** is a custom geospatial interface tailored specifically to how physical locations work in Zambia. It combines OpenStreetMap / Leaflet tile layers with high-contrast property status pins and local landmark descriptions.

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
Most residential subdivisions and commercial plots across Lusaka (Ibex Hill, New Kasama, Roma Park, Silverest, Chongwe) lack formalized street numbers. Global mapping apps frequently fail because Google Maps cannot resolve informal plot designations (*"Plot 418/A off Leopards Hill"*). Consequently, field agents spend 20 minutes on the phone guiding lost buyers: *"Turn right at the blue gate, drive past the borehole driller, then call me."* Viewing appointments are frequently missed or delayed.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **Leaflet & OpenStreetMap Engine**: Lightweight, open-source mapping stack with zero Google Maps API licensing costs and zero vendor lock-in.
- **Color-Coded Status Pins**:
  - 🔴 **Red**: Available for Sale
  - 🟡 **Yellow**: Available for Rent
  - 🟢 **Green**: Sold / Under Offer
  - 🔵 **Blue**: Tenanted / Rented
  - ⚪ **Gray**: Raw Land / Plot
- **Synchronized Card Slider**: Panning across the map dynamically filters the property card list below, highlighting listings currently inside the active map bounding box.
- **Dual-Location Landmark Engine**:
  - Every property record stores two location primitives: exact GPS coordinates (`latitude`, `longitude`) + a structured `landmarkDirections` text field.
  - Tapping **"Get Landmark Directions"** displays step-by-step turn guidance anchored to well-known Lusaka landmarks (e.g. *"100m past American Embassy off Kakola Road, opposite Catholic Church"*).
  - Tapping **"Open in Google/Apple Maps"** passes the exact GPS pin directly into the driver's phone navigation app.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **40% Reduction in Viewing No-Shows**: Buyers navigate straight to the plot without getting frustrated or turning back.
- **Instant Spatial Context**: When a buyer asks for properties near their children's school (e.g. Baobab or AIS), agents can visually circle the neighborhood on the map in real time.
- **Data Sovereignty**: OpenStreetMap tiles cache locally inside PowerSync SQLite for offline usage during network blackouts.
