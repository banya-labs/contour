# 01.2 — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Field Personas

> **Focus Segment**: Independent Real Estate Agencies & Property Management Firms in Southern Africa  
> **Initial Beachhead**: Lusaka, Zambia  

---

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Definition

Contour is purpose-built for mid-tier, independent real estate firms that have outgrown Excel and WhatsApp, but cannot afford or utilize heavy enterprise enterprise software like Salesforce.

### Primary ICP Characteristics:
- **Brokerage Size**: 5 to 40 active agents.
- **Inventory Size**: 30 to 300 active listings across sales and rentals.
- **Property Management Portfolio**: 20 to 500 residential and commercial rental units.
- **Geographic Footprint**: Based in major Southern African hubs (Lusaka, Harare, Johannesburg, Livingstone, Ndola, Kitwe).
- **Core Technology Today**: WhatsApp Business, manual Excel spreadsheets, personal smartphone cameras, and a WordPress Divi website.

### Persona 1: Grace — The Managing Director / Principal Broker
- **Profile**: Owner of a growing real estate agency in Rhodes Park, Lusaka. Responsible for company profitability, agent retention, client trust, and compliance.
- **Core Goals**:
  - Gain real-time visibility into monthly company cash flow, pending agency commissions, and rental arrears.
  - Eliminate internal commission disputes between agents.
  - Deliver professional, error-free monthly remittance statements to landlords on time.
- **Major Pain Points**:
  - Spends the last 4 days of every month manually reconciling bank statements across Kwacha and USD accounts.
  - Agents hide leads or poach each other's clients, creating internal friction.
  - Landlords call constantly demanding payout updates.
- **How Contour Wins Her Over**:
  - The **Daily Work Queue ("What Needs to Happen Today")** gives her an instant morning summary of leases expiring, overdue rent, and pending commissions.
  - 1-Click Landlord Remittance Statements with **The DocuSign Seam** cut monthly accounting from 4 days to 15 minutes.

---

### Persona 2: Tembo — The Field Real Estate Agent
- **Profile**: 32-year-old active agent. Spends 80% of his day in traffic, meeting buyers and landlords in Kabulonga, Roma, and Ibex Hill.
- **Core Goals**:
  - Close deals fast, protect his client leads, and get paid his commission splits without delays.
  - Send clean, attractive property info to prospective buyers over WhatsApp in seconds.
- **Major Pain Points**:
  - His phone gallery is clogged with thousands of unsorted property photos.
  - Forwards listings to colleagues only to have them go behind his back and steal his buyer.
  - When clients ask for directions, he has to type long text descriptions of landmarks repeatedly.
- **How Contour Wins Him Over**:
  - The **Field Agent Mobile PWA** allows him to open an offline Lusaka map, tap a property, and generate a branded WhatsApp flyer in 2 taps.
  - The **30-Day Anti-Poaching Lock** guarantees that once he registers a client, no other agent in his agency can steal that commission.

---

### Persona 3: Banda — The Property Owner / Landlord
- **Profile**: High-net-worth Zambian professional or diaspora investor based in the UK/South Africa owning 4 rental properties in Lusaka.
- **Core Goals**:
  - Receive net rental payouts on time every month with 100% transparent deductions.
- **Major Pain Points**:
  - Never knows if tenants paid on time or if the agency is sitting on his money.
  - Receives handwritten receipts or chaotic WhatsApp screenshots for maintenance costs (e.g. borehole repairs, plumbing).
- **How Contour Wins Him Over**:
  - Receives an automated, professional PDF statement every month showing:  
    $$\text{Gross Rent Collected} - 10\% \text{ Agency Fee} - \text{Audited Maintenance} = \text{Net Landlord Payout}$$
  - Maintenance costs include photos of original receipts and contractor sign-offs.

---

### Persona 4: Mwila — The Residential / Commercial Tenant
- **Profile**: Working professional renting a 3-bedroom townhouse in Sunningdale or a company leasing office space.
- **Core Goals**:
  - Simple payment channels, transparent rent receipts, and fast maintenance resolution.
- **Major Pain Points**:
  - Feels harassed when agents call aggressively on the 1st of the month, even when payment was initiated via bank transfer.
- **How Contour Wins Him Over**:
  - Automated, polite WhatsApp reminder notifications on Day 1, with instant confirmation receipts once payment clears.
