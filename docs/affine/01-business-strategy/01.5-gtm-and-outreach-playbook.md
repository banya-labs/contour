## 2. Cold B2B Outreach Script (WhatsApp & In-Person Broker Meetings)

In Zambia, cold email has poor conversion. Brokerage directors live on **WhatsApp and phone calls**, and respond best to direct operational pain points.

### WhatsApp Hook to Agency Principals / Managing Directors:

> *"Good morning [Director Name], hope you are having a productive week.*  
>  
> *I’m reaching out from Banya Labs here in Lusaka. We noticed [Agency Name] has an impressive portfolio of properties across Kabulonga and Leopards Hill.*  
>  
> *Most agency directors we speak with in Rhodes Park share the same 3 headaches:*  
> *1. Spending 3–4 days at the end of every month manually typing landlord remittance statements in Excel.*  
> *2. Chasing rental arrears manually across dozens of tenants over WhatsApp.*  
> *3. Field agents arguing over who brought which client or losing leads on the road.*  
>  
> *We built **Contour** specifically for Zambian brokerages. It automates your monthly landlord payout statements in 1 click, gives your field agents an offline Lusaka property map with 2-tap branded WhatsApp flyers, and locks client leads for 30 days to stop intra-agency poaching.*  
>  
> *We’re currently onboarding MAL’s Property Consultancy over on Joseph Mwilwa Road.*  
>  
> *Could I pass by your office for 10 minutes this Thursday to show you a quick live demo on mobile?"*

## 3. The 10-Minute Executive Pitch Structure

When meeting with the Principal Broker (Grace), follow this exact 4-step sequence:

1. **Step 1: The Landlord Statement Pain (Minutes 1–3)**:
   - *"How long does your team spend calculating gross rent, deducting 10% agency management commission, subtracting maintenance receipts, and preparing landlord payout slips?"*
   - Show Contour's **1-Click Landlord Remittance Generator** with automatic maintenance deduction tracking.
2. **Step 2: The Field Agent Demo (Minutes 4–6)**:
   - Hand the director a smartphone running Contour's **Field Agent Mobile PWA**.
   - Show the interactive Lusaka property map with landmark directions (*"150m off Leopards Hill Rd past AIS"*).
   - Tap once to generate a branded, masked-PII WhatsApp flyer and send it to her phone.
3. **Step 3: The 30-Day Anti-Poaching Lock (Minutes 7–8)**:
   - Explain how registering a client locks that buyer to the agent for 30 days.
   - Explain how this solves the broker's biggest headache: agents hiding high-value buyers in private chats.
4. **Step 4: The Pilot Offer (Minutes 9–10)**:
   - *"We will set up [Agency Name] with a full 30-day pilot on the Growth Tier. We’ll import your top 25 listings and train your field agents in 15 minutes. If it doesn't save your team 20 hours in the first month, you owe us nothing."*
