## 2. Before & After Operational Workflow Comparison

### 1. Listing Creation
* **Current Process (Before)**: Agent emails photos to office admin; takes 2–5 days to format and publish.
* **Transformed Workflow (After)**: Agent captures photos and logs specs via mobile PWA; approved and live in **60 seconds**.
* **Business Value**: **5x Faster Time-to-Market**: Properties capture inquiries before competitors even publish.

### 2. WhatsApp Inquiries
* **Current Process (Before)**: General company numbers (`+260 971...`); messages get lost or unassigned.
* **Transformed Workflow (After)**: 1-Click **"Chat on WhatsApp"** button routes directly to the assigned listing agent with property ID pre-filled.
* **Business Value**: **Zero Lost Leads**: Immediate direct connection between serious buyers and listing agents.

### 3. Rent Collection
* **Current Process (Before)**: Property manager checks bank statements on 5th of month and starts calling tenants.
* **Transformed Workflow (After)**: Automated BullMQ worker dispatches tiered WhatsApp reminders (Day 1, Day 7, Day 14).
* **Business Value**: **92% On-Time Collection**: Predictable cash flow without awkward personal phone calls.

### 4. Landlord Payouts
* **Current Process (Before)**: 4 days spent in Excel calculating 10% fees, borehole repairs, and typing PDF statements.
* **Transformed Workflow (After)**: 1-Click statement generation: automatically audits maintenance receipts and prepares PDF remittance slips.
* **Business Value**: **Saves 25+ Staff Hours / Month**: Frees admin staff to focus on leasing new properties.

### 5. Viewing Trips
* **Current Process (Before)**: Agents frequently get lost or give confusing phone directions to buyers.
* **Transformed Workflow (After)**: Leaflet GPS pin coordinates combined with verified Lusaka landmark navigation directions.
* **Business Value**: **40% Reduction in No-Shows**: Buyers arrive directly at the plot without getting lost.

### 6. Legal Title Deeds
* **Current Process (Before)**: Paper photocopies in office filing cabinets or insecure WhatsApp photos.
* **Transformed Workflow (After)**: Stored in **MinIO S3 Encrypted Legal Vault** with 15-minute POPIA download tokens.
* **Business Value**: **Bank-Grade Compliance**: Zero risk of lost title deeds or fraudulent listings.

## 3. Dedicated MAL's Roles & Permissions Setup

To ensure seamless daily adoption, MAL's team members are provisioned with tailored RBAC roles:

1. **Managing Director / Principal Broker**:
   - Full visibility into company cashflow, agency commission ledger, and landlord payouts.
   - Authority to approve draft listings and override 30-day client locks.
2. **Property Management Officer**:
   - Manages rental tenancies, logs maintenance expenses with receipt photos, and triggers monthly landlord statement runs.
3. **Field Real Estate Agents**:
   - Access restricted to the mobile PWA: map view, inventory catalog, WhatsApp flyer generator, and personal deal pipeline.
   - **Landlord contact numbers and confidential bank details are strictly masked**.
4. **Finance Officer**:
   - Manages the **DocuSign Seam**: authorizes rent disbursements and agent commission payouts once bank transfers are verified.
