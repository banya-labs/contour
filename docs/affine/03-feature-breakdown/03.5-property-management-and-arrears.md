# 03.5 — Property Management, Leases & Arrears Escalation

> **Surface Path**: `/dashboard/leases` & `/dashboard/properties`  
> **Primary Audience**: Property Managers, Tenancy Officers, Finance Staff  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Property Management & Arrears Module** governs the full lifecycle of residential and commercial tenancies across managed property portfolios. It tracks lease agreements, security deposits, monthly rent due dates, maintenance requests, and automates rent arrears recovery.

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
Rent arrears represent the single largest operational friction point for property management firms in Zambia. When tenants fall behind, property managers hesitate to chase them until weeks have passed. Tracking who paid into which bank account (ZMW vs USD) is chaotic. Manual WhatsApp messages sent by staff often sound either overly aggressive (damaging tenant relationships) or too timid (failing to collect), and there is no record of when notices were delivered.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **Comprehensive Tenancy Ledger**:
  - Distinguishes **Company-Owned Units** vs **Managed Units** (on behalf of third-party landlords).
  - Dual-currency terms: Tracks monthly rent in `ZMW` or `USD`, payment frequency (monthly, quarterly, bi-annually), security deposit held, and lease start/end dates.
- **Automated Rent Arrears Escalator (BullMQ Background Worker)**:
  - Daily scheduled cron monitors all active leases against rent due dates.
  - Generates tiered notifications:
    - **Tier 1 (Day 1 Overdue)**: Friendly reminder: *"Good morning [Tenant], this is a polite reminder from MAL's Property Consultancy that rent for [Property] was due on the 1st. Please find account details below..."*
    - **Tier 2 (Day 7 Overdue)**: Formal overdue notice stating late fee assessment.
    - **Tier 3 (Day 14 Overdue)**: Legal letter of demand and escalation to landlord.
- **Idempotent 4-Day Cooldown Lock**:
  - To prevent embarrassing duplicate messages or bot spam loops, the system enforces an immutable Redis key:
    `cooldown:arrears:{leaseId}:{year}:{month}:{tier}`
  - The worker will never send more than one notice per tier within a 4-day window.
- **Maintenance Deduction Tracking**:
  - Property managers log maintenance expenses (e.g. borehole pump repair, roof leak fix) with contractor invoice amounts and photo receipts.
  - Approved maintenance expenses are automatically held in deduction escrow for the next monthly landlord statement.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **92% On-Time Rent Collection**: Regular, professional, and automated WhatsApp notices dramatically improve tenant payment discipline.
- **Preserves Staff Morale**: Removes the emotional burden of personally chasing tenants from property management staff.
- **Complete Legal Defense**: Generates a timestamped audit trail of all notices delivered in the event of eviction proceedings.
