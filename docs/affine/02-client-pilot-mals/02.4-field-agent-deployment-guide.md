# 02.4 — Field Agent Deployment Guide: Rhodes Park, Kabulonga & Roma

> **Target End-User**: MAL's Real Estate Field Agents  
> **Deployment Surface**: Field Agent Mobile PWA (`https://contour.banyalabs.com/kiosk` or `/agent`)  
> **Operational Context**: On-the-road viewing trips across Lusaka's key residential and commercial corridors.

---

## 1. The Field Agent Mobility Problem

MAL's field agents spend 80% of their working hours driving between viewings in:
- **Rhodes Park & Fairview**: Commercial offices, embassy premises, and law firm properties.
- **Kabulonga & Sunningdale**: Prime diplomatic residences and executive townhouses.
- **Leopards Hill & New Kasama**: Large multi-acre estates and gated communities.
- **Roma Park & Mass Media**: Modern corporate headquarters and residential apartments.

During these trips, agents face:
1. **Network Load-Shedding & Blackspots**: 3G/4G connectivity drops frequently in suburban pockets or during ZESCO load-shedding.
2. **Unprofessional WhatsApp Forwarding**: When sending property specs to prospective clients, agents forward raw phone camera photos along with messy WhatsApp text, frequently leaking the owner's phone number or looking unpolished.
3. **Internal Distrust**: Agents fear logging high-net-worth clients into a central system because colleagues might snipe their commission.

### Tool 1: Offline GPS Property Map
- When an agent opens Contour on mobile, all active MAL's properties render on an interactive map:
  - 🔴 **Red Pin**: Available for Sale
  - 🟡 **Yellow Pin**: Available for Rent
  - 🟢 **Green Pin**: Sold / Under Offer
  - 🔵 **Blue Pin**: Rented
- Tapping a pin reveals high-res photos, price in ZMW/USD, and verified landmark directions.
- Because data is cached locally via **PowerSync SQLite**, the map functions flawlessly even in offline mode.

### Tool 2: 2-Tap Branded WhatsApp Listing Flyer
- When a prospective buyer on a viewing trip asks: *"Do you have anything similar in Kabulonga under $2,500?"*
- The agent filters by Kabulonga, taps **"Share Flyer"**, and Contour instantly generates a branded, professional card:
  - Includes MAL's Property Consultancy logo, agent's direct phone number, key property features, and price.
  - **Crucially: The property owner's phone number and bank details are 100% masked**.
- Tapping **"Send on WhatsApp"** immediately launches WhatsApp with the flyer ready to send.

### Tool 3: 30-Day Client Anti-Poaching Lock
- After the viewing, the agent taps **"Register Client"**, entering the buyer's name, phone number, and budget.
- That client is locked to the agent for 30 days across MAL's entire team.
- If the buyer later calls MAL's office landline or speaks to another agent, the system attributes the deal to the original agent, guaranteeing their 25% closing commission.

## 3. 15-Minute Field Agent Onboarding Checklist

Follow this simple checklist to onboard MAL's agents:

- [ ] **Step 1: PWA Installation (2 mins)**:
  - Agent opens `https://contour.banyalabs.com` in mobile Safari or Chrome.
  - Taps **"Add to Home Screen"**. Contour installs as a standalone native app icon with zero App Store download.
- [ ] **Step 2: Dev Mode / Fast Login (1 min)**:
  - Agent logs in using their MAL's work email and selects the `FIELD_AGENT` persona.
- [ ] **Step 3: Test WhatsApp Flyer Export (3 mins)**:
  - Agent selects a Kabulonga townhouse listing.
  - Taps **"Export WhatsApp Card"** and verifies that their name and phone number appear correctly alongside MAL's logo.
- [ ] **Step 4: Register First Client Lead (4 mins)**:
  - Agent registers a test buyer inquiry to see the 30-day anti-poaching lock in action.
- [ ] **Step 5: Test Offline Mode (5 mins)**:
  - Toggle smartphone to Airplane Mode.
  - Open Contour and confirm the Lusaka map and cached listing specs remain fully readable.
