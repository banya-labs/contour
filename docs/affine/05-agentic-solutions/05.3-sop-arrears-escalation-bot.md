# 05.3 — Operational SOP: Idempotent Arrears Escalation Bot

> **Agent Code**: `CONTOUR-OPS-02`  
> **Runtime**: Redis BullMQ Cron Worker + WhatsApp Gateway  
> **Human Supervisor**: Property Management Officer  

---

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This background worker executes scheduled rent arrears scans across all active tenancies. It identifies overdue rent payments and dispatches polite, professional, and audited WhatsApp reminders according to a strict 3-tier escalation schedule, backed by an idempotent 4-day cooldown key.

---

## 2. Escalation Tier Schedule

### 🟢 Tier 1: Friendly Nudge
* **Trigger Window**: 1 to 5 days overdue.
* **Tone & Content**: Courteous reminder; assumes oversight; provides bank details.
* **Delivery Channel**: WhatsApp Direct.

### 🟡 Tier 2: Overdue Notice
* **Trigger Window**: 6 to 13 days overdue.
* **Tone & Content**: Formal notice stating late penalty clause; requests payment slip.
* **Delivery Channel**: WhatsApp + Email.

### 🔴 Tier 3: Final Notice
* **Trigger Window**: 14+ days overdue.
* **Tone & Content**: Legal demand letter; notifies of landlord escalation & lease review.
* **Delivery Channel**: WhatsApp + Registered Letter.

---

## 3. The 4-Day Cooldown Lock (Anti-Spam Invariant)

To protect tenant relationships and prevent runaway bot dispatch loops:
1. Before transmitting any message, the worker checks Redis for an atomic lock key:
   `cooldown:arrears:{leaseId}:{year}:{month}:{tier}`
2. If the key exists (TTL = 4 days = 345,600 seconds), the execution is aborted immediately with log:
   `[ARREARS_COOLDOWN_ACTIVE] Lease ${leaseId} is currently under cooldown for tier ${tier}.`
3. Once sent, the key is written with `SET ... EX 345600`.

---

## 5. Audit Logging & Human Escalation
- Every sent reminder is recorded in the `ArrearsNoticeLog` table.
- If a tenant replies to the WhatsApp message with keywords (*"dispute"*, *"broke"*, *"maintenance"*, *"refund"*), the bot immediately sets the conversation status to `open` (escalating to human staff) and halts automated follow-ups.
