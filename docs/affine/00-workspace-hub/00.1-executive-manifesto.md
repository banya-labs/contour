## 1. Executive Summary & The Venture Manifesto

Real estate in Southern Africa does not move through glossy desktop web portals. It moves through **informal WhatsApp group chats, dusty roadside viewing trips, cash deposits, mobile money, and word-of-mouth broker networks**.

Yet, every real estate agency in Lusaka, Harare, and Johannesburg has been forced to choose between two broken extremes:
1. **Expensive Western CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce, Propertybase)**: Built for standardized US/UK addresses, formal MLS syndication, and single-currency credit-card environments. They treat gross property sale values ($200,000) as company revenue, fail completely on Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) dual-currency leases, cannot handle landmark-based directions, and suffer immediate data entry strikes from field agents who live on WhatsApp.
2. **Fragile Custom WordPress Stacks (Divi + Real Estate Plugins)**: Prone to database bloat, plugin breaks, disconnected field agents, and zero financial accounting. Management ends up spending the last 4 days of every month manually reconciling bank slips and typing landlord payout statements in Excel.

**Contour was engineered to bridge this exact divide.**

Contour is a **Multi-Surface Real Estate Operating System** that unifies the back-office brokerage ledger with the front-line mobile reality of the field agent. It provides:
- **For Management**: True net commission accounting (5% agency splits), automated rent arrears escalation, 1-click audited landlord remittance statements, and multi-tenant brokerage governance.
- **For Field Agents**: High-contrast, low-latency mobile PWA with offline GPS landmark mapping, 2-tap branded WhatsApp listing flyers (with masked landlord PII), and a 30-day anti-poaching client lock.
- **For Property Owners & Landlords**: Complete transparency over rental yields, net remittances, and verified maintenance deductions.
- **For External AI & Ecosystem Agents**: An authenticated JSON-RPC 2.0 Model Context Protocol (MCP) tool grid at `/api/mcp`.

## 2. Strategic Context: The Banya Thesis

> *"The infrastructure is the thesis. Each vertical is the proof."*

Contour is built on the **Banya Labs Boilerplate v4.0** architecture, adhering to 4 non-negotiable operational principles:
1. **Readiness Beats Hype. Sequence Beats Sprinting**: Real estate agents do not need autonomous speculative AI buying houses. They need fast, reliable digital labor: automated arrears reminders, audio note listing extraction, and instant flyer generation with human authorization gates (**The DocuSign Seam**).
2. **Finite Finishes Beat Infinite Starts ("Do Less, Better")**: Rather than building generic multi-industry software, Contour is laser-focused on the exact operational workflow of Lusaka brokerages—starting with our pilot client, **MAL's Property Consultancy**.
3. **Multi-Surface Separation**: Human surfaces (Dashboard, Field PWA, Public Cards) and Machine surfaces (MCP tools, semantic contracts) co-exist on a shared, tenant-isolated PostgreSQL and MinIO S3 foundation.
4. **Southern African Resilience**:
   - Dual-currency native (`ZMW` and `USD`).
   - Offline-first execution via **PowerSync** local SQLite WASM to withstand daily ZESCO power load-shedding and spotty mobile network coverage.
   - Paystack payment gateway supporting cards and regional **Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Airtel Money)**.

## 3. Core North Star Metrics

Contour's success across our client network is governed by 5 non-negotiable operational metrics:

### 1. Net Commission Velocity
* **Target Standard**: Under 48 hours from deal sign to agent payout.
* **Operational Impact**: Eliminates intra-agency financial disputes and motivates agent performance.

### 2. On-Time Rent Collection Rate
* **Target Standard**: > 92% across all managed units.
* **Operational Impact**: Driven by automated tiered WhatsApp arrears reminders with 4-day cooldown locks.

### 3. Landlord Statement Generation Time
* **Target Standard**: Under 3 minutes per month per property.
* **Operational Impact**: Replaces 20+ hours of manual spreadsheet reconciliation with 1-click audited statements.

### 4. Field Agent Adoption Rate
* **Target Standard**: > 85% of active listings logged via mobile/WhatsApp.
* **Operational Impact**: Zero desktop data entry friction; agents log leads in 2 taps.

### 5. Tenant Isolation & Zero PII Leakage
* **Target Standard**: 100% compliance with regional data sovereignty.
* **Operational Impact**: Landlord direct contact details and Title Deeds are masked on mobile surfaces and locked behind 15-minute POPIA download tokens.
