# 03.1 — Public Marketing Surface & Shareable Listing Engine

> **Surface Path**: `/` (Public Landing Page) & `/p/[slug]` (Public Property Cards)  
> **Primary Audience**: Prospective Property Buyers, Tenants, Landlords, and Agency Leads  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Public Marketing Surface** is Contour's high-speed public storefront. It serves two distinct functions:
1. An exceptional, responsive marketing landing page that communicates Contour's value proposition, dual-currency pricing, and ROI calculator to prospective brokerage clients.
2. Individual, standalone property listing cards (`/p/[slug]`) designed for high-conversion sharing across WhatsApp, Facebook Marketplace, and email.

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
Standard real estate websites in Zambia are slow, heavy, and fail to convert mobile data users. They present static contact forms (*"Submit an inquiry and our team will get back to you in 48 hours"*). In Southern Africa, prospective buyers and tenants will **never** fill out long forms; they expect immediate WhatsApp communication with a human agent. Furthermore, standard listing pages show rigid street addresses that confuse buyers.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **Next.js 14 App Router Server Components**: Pages are pre-rendered at the edge for sub-second load times on 3G mobile data.
- **Dynamic Dual-Currency Switcher**: Displays prices seamlessly in both `ZMW` and `USD`.
- **Integrated Leaflet Map with Landmark Breadcrumbs**: Shows interactive GPS pins alongside verified local landmark directions (*"150m off Ibex Hill Road near Twin Palm Mall"*).
- **1-Click WhatsApp Direct Routing**: Prominent floating action button:
  `https://wa.me/{agentPhone}?text=Hi%20{agentName},%20I'm%20inquiring%20about%20{propertyTitle}%20(ID:%20{propertyId})`
  Pre-populates the WhatsApp message so the agent immediately knows which property the client is looking at.
- **OpenGraph & Twitter Card Meta Tags**: When a link is pasted into WhatsApp or Facebook, it renders an eye-catching photo preview, verified title, and price.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **3x Higher Conversion Rate**: Eliminates friction; inquiries connect directly to agents within 5 seconds.
- **Zero Form Abandonment**: Replaces obsolete 5-field inquiry forms with instant conversational commerce.
- **Instant Brand Elevation**: Gives independent agencies a sleek, modern, Silicon Valley-grade public surface.
