### 6. The Problem Statement (The 3 Deadly Agency Leaks)
1. **The "Gross Value" Illusion**: Your pipeline shows $2,000,000 in listings, but your bank account is dry because generic CRMs don't track true 5% earned agency commissions or agent payout splits.
2. **The "WhatsApp Black Hole"**: Your agents have 40 listing photos buried in phone galleries. When a qualified buyer calls, it takes 3 hours to find out if the property was already rented last week.
3. **The Landlord Reconciliation Nightmare**: Spending the first 5 days of every month manually compiling Excel statements to deduct your 10% fee and maintenance before wiring net rent to landlords.

---

### 7. The Solution (How Contour Fixes It)
- **Live Financial Ledger**: Automatically separates gross asset value from your actual agency fee and agent commission splits.
- **Landmark-Ready GPS Map**: Visual color-coded pins (🔴 For Sale, 🟡 For Rent, 🟢 Sold) with local landmark directions for Lusaka viewings.
- **1-Click Landlord Remittance Statements**: Automatically reconcile collected rent, deduct management fees, and generate branded PDF statements in seconds.
- **WhatsApp Field Ingestion**: Agents draft listings and send inquiries directly from WhatsApp.

---

### 8. Feature Deep Dive (4 Core Pillars)
1. **Dual-Inventory Engine**: Seamlessly manage Company-Owned development plots vs. Third-Party Managed landlord stock.
2. **Automated Rent Arrears Watcher**: Polite, automated WhatsApp reminders dispatched to tenants on Day 1, Day 7, and Day 14.
3. **Field Agent PWA**: Works offline during load-shedding with local caching of photos and client contact locks.
4. **Agent Anti-Poaching Lock**: Locks client inquiries to the introducing agent for 30 days to eliminate internal commission disputes.

### 11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- **Q: How does Contour handle properties with no formal street address in Lusaka?**  
  *A: Contour uses precise GPS pin-dropping combined with a dedicated "Landmark Directions" field (e.g., "150m off Leopards Hill Rd past American International School"), ensuring field agents navigate directly to the gate.*
- **Q: Can my agents see landlord contact numbers?**  
  *A: No. Contour implements strict field-level RBAC. Field agents see property specs, lockbox notes, and viewing hours, while landlord personal numbers and bank details remain locked to Management.*
- **Q: Does Contour support both Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) and US Dollars (USD)?**  
  *A: Yes. You can price individual listings in ZMW or USD with automatic exchange rate tracking.*
