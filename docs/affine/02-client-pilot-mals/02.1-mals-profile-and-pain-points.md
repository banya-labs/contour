## 1. Company Background & Service Footprint

**MAL’s Property Consultancy** is a recognized Lusaka real estate agency operating out of Rhodes Park. They provide four core services to the Zambian market:

1. **Property Management**: Managing residential apartment blocks, standalone executive houses, and commercial premises across Lusaka; collecting rent, handling tenant relations, and coordinating physical maintenance.
2. **Sales and Leasing**: Facilitating the sale and rental of residential estates, commercial office space, and agricultural farm blocks in Lusaka and surrounding regions.
3. **Property Valuation**: Assisting property owners, financial institutions, and legal estates with certified property valuation appraisals.
4. **Tenant and Landlord Representation**: Acting as dedicated fiduciaries for property owners negotiating long-term lease terms and corporate leases.

## 2. Technical Audit of Current Website (`malsproperty.co.zm`)

A deep architectural analysis of MAL's current web footprint reveals:
- **CMS & Theme**: WordPress 6.x powered by **Divi Theme (v4.27.6)** with custom Divi Builder sections.
- **Real Estate Engine**: **Essential Real Estate (ERE)** WordPress plugin (`ere_main-css`, `ere_map-css`, Bootstrap 4.6.2).
- **Interactive Mapping**: Standard Google Maps JS API script with generic lat/lng pin popups, lacking landmark directions and offline caching.
- **User Authentication**: Standard WordPress/ERE popup modal (`#ere_signin_modal`) requiring user email and password, which field agents rarely use.
- **Lead Capture**: Static phone numbers and email links; lacks dynamic WhatsApp click-to-chat triggers attached to specific agents and listing IDs.

## 3. Core Operational Pain Points Experienced by MAL's

Through our on-the-ground operational survey, MAL's faces 4 critical operational bottlenecks:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 MAL'S PROPERTY CONSULTANCY OPERATIONAL BOTTLENECKS          │
├──────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Field Disconnect      │ Agents in Kabulonga/Roma take listing photos on  │
│                          │ phones; listings sit in WhatsApp for days before │
│                          │ ever being uploaded to malsproperty.co.zm.       │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. Month-End Statement   │ Admin staff spends 4 days manually reconciling   │
│    Gridlock              │ bank deposits, deducting 10% agency commissions, │
│                          │ subtracting borehole/paint repairs in Excel.     │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. Unaudited Rent        │ Chasing overdue tenants relies on memory and     │
│    Collections           │ ad-hoc WhatsApp calls; no systematic escalation  │
│                          │ or audit trail of arrears notices.               │
├──────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. Client Poaching &     │ High-value buyers and tenants are kept in agents'│
│    Information Silos     │ private chats; management cannot see the true    │
│                          │ deal pipeline or forecast cash flow.             │
└──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **The Field-to-Website Ingestion Lag**:
   - MAL's agents take photos and videos on viewing trips in Rhodes Park, Kabulonga, or Leopards Hill.
   - Because the WordPress Divi admin portal is clunky and desktop-heavy, agents don't upload listings themselves. They email or WhatsApp photos to office staff, causing multi-day delays before prospective buyers see listings on the website.
2. **Month-End Landlord Remittance Reconciliations**:
   - At the end of every calendar month, MAL's finance staff must calculate:
     $$\text{Gross Rent Collected} - 10\% \text{ Agency Management Fee} - \text{Plumber/Electrician Maintenance} = \text{Net Landlord Payout}$$
   - When done in spreadsheets across dozens of landlords, errors occur, receipts are lost, and landlord payouts are delayed, causing frustration.
3. **Rent Arrears Chasing Chaos**:
   - Tracking which tenants paid on the 1st vs who is late on the 7th requires cross-referencing multiple bank accounts. Property managers feel uncomfortable making aggressive calls without an objective, professional escalation trail.
4. **No Offline Field Mobility**:
   - When MAL's agents are showing rural plots or properties in newly subdivided areas with poor cell coverage, the WordPress site fails to load, leaving agents without key property specs and plot boundaries.
