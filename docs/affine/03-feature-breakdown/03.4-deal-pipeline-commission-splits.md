# 03.4 — Deal Pipeline Kanban & 5% Commission Split Ledger

> **Surface Path**: `/dashboard/pipeline` & `/dashboard/sales`  
> **Primary Audience**: Managing Brokers, Sales Directors, Listing & Closing Agents  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Deal Pipeline Kanban** provides a visual, drag-and-drop workflow tracking property sales from initial buyer offer through legal conveyancing, title deed transfer, and final commission payout. It is backed by a deterministic **5% Commission Split Ledger** that calculates house cuts vs agent payouts in real time.

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
Property sales in Southern Africa take between 30 and 120 days to close due to Ministry of Lands title searches, state consent requirements, and conveyancing lawyers holding funds in escrow. Because tracking is done verbally or on whiteboards, deals frequently stall at lawyers without the agency noticing. When deals finally close, arguments break out over who is owed what percentage of the commission, leading to agent resignations and toxic office politics.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **Interactive Kanban Board**: Drag-and-drop cards organized into 5 pipeline columns:
  1. `Offer Made` (Verbal/written offer submitted to seller)
  2. `Contract Executed` (Formal contract of sale signed)
  3. `Lawyers Escrow / Deposit Paid` (Buyer deposit deposited into conveyancing trust account)
  4. `Title Transfer / State Consent` (Deed lodged at Ministry of Lands)
  5. `Closed & Paid Out` (Commission funds settled)
- **Ministry of Lands Reference Tracking**: Every deal card records the formal Ministry Lands Reference Number, conveyancing law firm name, and responsible conveyancer.
- **Deterministic 3-Way Commission Split Engine**:
  - Automatically calculates:
    - **Gross Agency Commission** (5% of sale value)
    - **Agency Retained House Cut** (e.g. 50%)
    - **Listing Agent Payout** (e.g. 25% for bringing the mandate)
    - **Closing Agent Payout** (e.g. 25% for bringing the buyer)
- **Audit Verification & Receipt Lock**: Payouts cannot transition to `Closed & Paid Out` without an uploaded bank transfer receipt or transaction reference, providing complete fiscal accountability.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **Accelerates Deal Velocity by 35%**: Visual bottleneck tracking alerts management whenever a deal remains stuck at the lawyers for more than 14 days.
- **Zero Commission Disputes**: Mathematical certainty; agents see their exact commission split displayed on the deal card from day one.
- **Accurate Agency Valuation**: The brokerage maintains a verifiable historical audit trail of transaction volumes and commission earnings.
