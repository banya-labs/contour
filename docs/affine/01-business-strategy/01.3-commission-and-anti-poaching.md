# 01.3 — Commission Enablement & 30-Day Anti-Poaching Lock

> **Core Insight**: Real estate agencies in Southern Africa do not fail from a lack of properties; they fail from internal distrust, unrecorded commission splits, and client poaching between agents.

---

## 1. The 5% Net Commission Lifecycle Engine

In Southern Africa, the industry-standard agency commission on property sales is **5% of the gross transaction value**, and **10% of monthly gross rent** on managed rental units.

International CRMs treat the gross property value as company revenue. Contour treats the **Net Earned Commission** as the primary financial asset, tracking it through a 5-stage deterministic state machine:

```
┌────────────┐     ┌───────────┐     ┌────────────────────┐     ┌──────────┐     ┌─────────────────┐
│  EXPECTED  │ ──► │  EARNED   │ ──► │ PARTIALLY RECEIVED │ ──► │ RECEIVED │ ──► │ AGENT PAID OUT  │
└────────────┘     └───────────┘     └────────────────────┘     └──────────┘     └─────────────────┘
  Offer Made         Contract          Deposit Clears             Full Funds        Split Transferred
  & Accepted          Signed             at Lawyers                in Escrow            to Agent
```

### Stage Transitions:
1. **Expected**: Offer made by buyer and accepted by seller; formal sale agreement drafted. Commission is estimated.
2. **Earned**: Sale agreement executed; legal mandate locked with Ministry of Lands reference.
3. **Partially Received**: Initial buyer deposit (typically 10% or 20%) paid into conveyancing lawyers' trust account.
4. **Received**: Full purchase funds settled; title transfer completed; 5% agency commission deposited into agency bank account.
5. **Agent Paid Out**: Agency accounting calculates the internal splits and pays out the agent via bank transfer or mobile money.

## 2. Dynamic Commission Split Calculations

Contour automatically calculates three-way splits on every deal, eliminating manual spreadsheet calculations:

$$\text{Gross Sale Value} = \$200,000$$
$$\text{Gross Agency Commission (5\%)} = \$10,000$$

### 🏢 Brokerage House Cut (50%)
* **Calculated Payout**: **$5,000**
* **Role & Allocation**: Retained by agency for overhead, marketing, office, and software.

### 👤 Listing Agent (Tembo) (25%)
* **Calculated Payout**: **$2,500**
* **Role & Allocation**: Agent who sourced the property mandate from the landlord/seller.

### 🤝 Closing Agent (Sarah) (25%)
* **Calculated Payout**: **$2,500**
* **Role & Allocation**: Agent who brought the qualified buyer and negotiated the sale.

*Note: If Tembo is both the listing and closing agent, he earns the full 50% ($5,000).*

## 3. The 30-Day Client Anti-Poaching Lock

### The Operational Problem:
In most Lusaka brokerages, agents refuse to put their high-value client leads into a shared CRM. Why? Because other agents in the office will see the client's phone number and budget, call them directly with other listings, and steal the deal. Agents hoard leads in their personal WhatsApp chats or paper notebooks, leading to lost follow-ups and zero agency oversight.

### The Contour Mechanism:
1. **First-to-Register Protection**:
   - When an agent (Tembo) meets a new client, he logs them into Contour with name, phone number, budget range, and preferred locations.
   - The system initiates an immutable **30-Day Anti-Poaching Lock** linking that client exclusively to Tembo.
2. **Intra-Agency Protection Shield**:
   - If another agent (Sarah) attempts to create a deal or register an inquiry for that same client phone number, Contour blocks the operation:  
     > *"⚠️ Client Locked: This client is actively protected under Tembo's representation until October 15, 2026. Any deal closed with this client must credit Tembo as the procuring agent."*
3. **Activity-Based Lock Extension**:
   - Every time Tembo logs a viewing trip, sends a WhatsApp property flyer, or records an updated offer, the 30-day timer resets.
   - If 30 days pass with zero logged activity, the lock expires and the client enters the agency's shared pool.
4. **Manager Override**:
   - The Managing Broker (Grace) retains emergency override authority to reassign dormant or disputed leads.

### Quantifiable Business Impact:
- **100% Lead Capture**: Agents eagerly log every prospective buyer and tenant into Contour because doing so secures their legal claim to the commission.
- **Zero Intra-Agency Deal Sniping**: Fosters a collaborative team culture where agents freely share inventory knowing their buyer representation is protected.
