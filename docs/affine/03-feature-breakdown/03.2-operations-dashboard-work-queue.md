# 03.2 — Operations Dashboard & The Daily Work Queue

> **Surface Path**: `/dashboard` (Executive Command Center)  
> **Primary Audience**: Managing Directors, Principal Brokers, Property Managers  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Contour Operations Dashboard** is the command center for the entire agency. Instead of presenting overwhelming statistical graphs that paralyze decision-making, it is anchored around an operational concept: **The Daily Work Queue ("What Needs to Happen Today")**.

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
Agency directors and property managers arrive at the office with no clear visibility into what requires immediate attention. Leases expire without renewal discussions; tenants fall into 14-day arrears without notice; lawyer conveyancing deposits sit in escrow without follow-up; and commission payouts are delayed. Managers spend their mornings asking agents for verbal updates or combing through WhatsApp chats.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **The Daily Work Queue Engine**: An automated query runner that aggregates urgent items across 4 operational domains:
  1. **🚨 Rental Arrears Alert**: Lists all tenants who are 5+ days overdue with 1-click **"Send WhatsApp Nudge"** action.
  2. **📅 Leases Expiring in 30 Days**: Highlights upcoming lease expirations to trigger renewal negotiations before vacancies occur.
  3. **⚖️ Lawyer Trust Account Follow-Ups**: Deals in `Partially Received` state requiring conveyancing updates from lawyers.
  4. **💰 Pending Landlord Statement Approvals**: Reconciled statements awaiting manager click-authorization.
- **Cash Flow KPI Strip**: Real-time cards displaying:
  - Total Monthly Gross Rent Collected (USD & ZMW)
  - Net Agency 10% Management Revenue
  - Earned 5% Sales Commissions Pipeline
  - Active Managed Units Occupancy Rate (e.g. 94% Occupied)
- **Fast Persona & Org Switcher**: Built-in header component enabling 1-click switching between tenant organizations and test personas during development and demos.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **Zero Slipped Deadlines**: Every overdue payment, expiring lease, and pending payout is surfaced immediately.
- **60-Minute Morning Triage**: Cuts morning management status meetings from 60 minutes to an instant 3-minute glance.
- **Executive Peace of Mind**: Agency owners always know their exact bankable cash flow and financial health.
