# 06.4 — Regional Landmines & Mitigations Database

> **Institutional Memory**: Core operational risks, edge cases, and field landmines encountered in Southern African real estate operations, paired with Contour's automated safeguards.

---

## 1. Regional Landmines & Safeguards Registry

### 🛡️ LM-01: Fraudulent Title Deeds & Double-Selling of Plots
* **Category**: Legal & Identity
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Fraudulent title deeds and double-selling of plots across Lusaka suburbs.
* **Root Cause**: Paper photocopies floating in filing cabinets or unverified WhatsApp scans.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **MinIO S3 Vault**: Encrypted custody, 15-minute POPIA presigned download tokens, and mandatory Ministry of Lands reference tracking on all closed deals.

### 🛡️ LM-02: Gross Sale Recorded as Revenue (Tax Chaos)
* **Category**: Finance & Commission
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Booking the full property price as brokerage revenue, causing disastrous ZRA tax audits.
* **Root Cause**: US/European CRM assumptions that log $200k gross price instead of the 5% agency commission.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **5% Commission State Machine**: Strictly tracks net earned agency commission ($10,000) with automatic 3-way split ledger (Agency, Listing Agent, Closing Agent).

### 🛡️ LM-03: Currency Fluctuations (USD Leases vs Kwacha Expenses)
* **Category**: Currency & Cashflow
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Currency swings between USD-pegged leases and local Kwacha operating costs.
* **Root Cause**: Single-currency database assumptions in global SaaS apps.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **Native Dual-Currency Engine**: Fixed contract exchange rate locking on leases and automated statement conversions.

### 🛡️ LM-04: Non-Existent Street Numbers & Viewing No-Shows
* **Category**: Geospatial & Navigation
* **Regional Operational Risk**: High client viewing no-show rates because Lusaka plots lack formal street addresses.
* **Root Cause**: Standard Google Maps street routing failure in developing suburbs.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **Dual-Location Navigator**: High-precision Leaflet GPS coordinates paired with verified local landmark directions.

### 🛡️ LM-05: ZESCO Load-Shedding & Spotty Mobile Networks
* **Category**: Field Mobility
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Field agents unable to load property specs or take client notes during daily 8-hour power cuts.
* **Root Cause**: Heavy cloud-only web applications that freeze without active internet.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **PowerSync Offline Engine**: Local SQLite WASM replication ensuring instantaneous 0ms offline map and catalog browsing.

### 🛡️ LM-06: Intra-Agency Deal Sniping & Hidden Leads
* **Category**: Sales Operations
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Agents refusing to enter buyer leads into a shared CRM for fear of colleagues stealing the deal.
* **Root Cause**: Open shared client databases with unmasked contact info.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **30-Day Anti-Poaching Lock**: First-to-register protection links commission attribution to the procuring agent for 30 days.

### 🛡️ LM-07: Landlord Direct Contact & Bank Details Leaked
* **Category**: Data Privacy & POPIA
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Unscrupulous buyers bypassing the brokerage to contact landlords directly after seeing a flyer.
* **Root Cause**: Unedited PDF flyers forwarded directly on WhatsApp.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **Strict PII Masking Engine**: Automatically strips landlord contacts and legal names from all client-facing mobile flyers.

### 🛡️ LM-08: Harassing Rent Reminders & Tenant Escalations
* **Category**: Communications
* **Regional Operational Risk**: Broken automated scripts sending duplicate late notices, destroying tenant relationships.
* **Root Cause**: Naive cron jobs without idempotency locks.
* **Contour Safeguard**: **Idempotent 4-Day Cooldown**: Redis lock key blocks duplicate arrears notices within a 4-day window.
