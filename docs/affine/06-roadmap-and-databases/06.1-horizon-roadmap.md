## 2. Milestone Deliverables & Gate Criteria

### Horizon 1 Deliverables (Completed / Ready to Ship):
- Multi-surface architecture: Marketing, Dashboard, Map View, Leases, Sales, Statements, Field PWA.
- Lusaka GPS Landmark Navigator with verified location pins.
- Better Auth tenant foundation and fast dev login switcher.

### Horizon 2 Deliverables (Current Sprint):
- Finalize public landing page and pricing table.
- Wire Paystack ZMW/USD subscription gateway with Dev Mode toggle.
- Deploy embeddable catalog widget for `malsproperty.co.zm`.
- Conduct on-site training with MAL's field agents in Rhodes Park.

### Horizon 3 Deliverables (Next 60 Days):
- Expand to 15 top-tier Lusaka agencies across Kabulonga, Mass Media, and Roma.
- Turnkey self-serve onboarding: Brokerages sign up, upload their logo, and start logging listings in 5 minutes.
