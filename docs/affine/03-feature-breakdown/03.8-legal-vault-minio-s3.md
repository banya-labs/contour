# 03.8 — Legal Vault & MinIO S3 Encrypted Custody

> **Surface Path**: `/dashboard/documents`  
> **Primary Audience**: Legal Counsel, Conveyancing Officers, Principal Brokers  
> **Infrastructure Standard**: Self-Hosted MinIO S3 Object Storage + 15-Minute POPIA Presigned URLs  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Legal Documents Vault** provides encrypted, partitioned, and audited digital custody for sensitive real estate legal instruments: Certificates of Title, National Registration Card (NRC) / Passport ID scans, Landlord Exclusive Mandates, Cadastral Survey Diagrams, and Tenancy Agreements.

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
Real estate fraud is a severe issue in Southern Africa. Double-selling of plots and fraudulent title deeds occur when agencies lack strict custody of ownership documents. Today, photocopies of 99-year state leases and landlord NRC cards sit in unlocked filing cabinets or circulate freely in agency WhatsApp chats. If an agent's phone is lost or stolen, confidential landlord identity documents leak, violating South African POPIA and regional privacy laws and exposing the agency to legal liability.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **Dedicated S3 Storage Engine (MinIO)**:
  - Contour uses self-hosted, S3-compatible **MinIO** object storage rather than bloated database blobs (pushing back against MongoDB binary storage anti-patterns).
  - Multi-tenant path partitioning:
    `s3://contour-vault/{organizationId}/{category}/{timestamp}_{filename}`
- **15-Minute POPIA Presigned Download Tokens**:
  - Files in the Legal Vault are **never** publicly accessible.
  - When an authorized user (Managing Broker or Legal Counsel) requests to view a Certificate of Title, the server generates a cryptographically signed HMAC presigned URL valid for **exactly 15 minutes**.
  - After 15 minutes, the URL expires, preventing unauthorized link sharing or scraping.
- **Client-to-Storage Direct Presigned Uploads**:
  - High-resolution documents (e.g. 20MB scanned survey diagrams) upload directly from the browser to MinIO S3 via presigned PUT URLs, bypassing the Next.js web application server and eliminating memory starvation.
- **Immutable Audit Logging**:
  - Every document upload, view, download, and deletion is recorded in the `AuditLog` table with user ID, tenant ID, IP address, and timestamp.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **100% Legal Title Integrity**: Guarantees that only verified, mandates with authentic title deeds can be listed for sale.
- **POPIA & Regional Privacy Compliance**: Protects sensitive national identity documents from unauthorized mobile leaks.
- **Zero Server RAM Bottlenecks**: Presigned streaming ensures the web application remains fast and responsive regardless of document file sizes.
