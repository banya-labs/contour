# 03.6 — Landlord Statements & The DocuSign Seam

> **Surface Path**: `/dashboard/statements`  
> **Primary Audience**: Managing Directors, Finance Officers, Property Owners / Landlords  
> **Core Principle**: "Read the financials, don't wire the money." (The Human-System Seam)  

---

## 1. Feature Definition & Overview

The **Landlord Statement Engine** automates the generation of monthly rental remittance statements. It calculates gross collected rent, deducts the contractual agency management commission (e.g. 10%), subtracts verified property maintenance costs, and outputs a net landlord payout statement protected by a human authorization gate (**The DocuSign Seam**).

---

## 2. Problem Solved, Mechanism & Quantifiable Benefit

### 1. The Regional Problem Solved:
At the end of every month, agency admin staff spend 3 to 5 full working days manually building Excel sheets for dozens of property owners. Errors frequently occur: an agency fee is miscalculated, a plumber's invoice is forgotten or double-counted, or currency exchange rates are mixed up between USD leases and Kwacha repairs. Landlords receive unformatted, confusing spreadsheets, sparking disputes, distrust, and threats to move their portfolio to rival agencies.

### 2. The Architectural Mechanism:
- **Deterministic Reconciliation Formula**:
  $$\text{Net Landlord Payout} = \text{Gross Rent Collected} - \text{Agency Management Fee (10\%)} - \sum \text{Approved Maintenance Expenses}$$
- **The DocuSign Seam (Human Approval Gate)**:
  - Contour strictly forbids autonomous AI or automated scripts from executing financial payouts or issuing finalized tax invoices without explicit human sign-off.
  - The system compiles the draft statement, matches rent bank slips against maintenance invoices, and flags anomalies (e.g. maintenance expenses exceeding 25% of gross rent).
  - The Finance Officer or Principal Broker reviews the breakdown and clicks **"Authorize & Lock Statement"**.
- **1-Click PDF Statement Generation**:
  - Generates a PDF statement featuring the agency's corporate logo (e.g. MAL's Property Consultancy), property address, tenant payment dates, breakdown of fees, and attached photos of contractor maintenance receipts.
- **Automated WhatsApp / Email Dispatch**:
  - Once authorized, Contour provides a 1-click button to dispatch the statement directly to the landlord's WhatsApp number or email address.

### 3. Quantifiable Business Benefit:
- **Cuts Month-End Accounting Time by 85%**: Reduces statement generation from 4 days of manual spreadsheet labor to under 15 minutes.
- **100% Landlord Trust & Retention**: Transparent, audited statements with embedded maintenance receipts eliminate landlord disputes entirely.
- **Audited Financial History**: Creates an immutable fiscal record for annual tax filings and corporate audits.
